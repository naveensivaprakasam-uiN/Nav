Brand Brain voice capture

Records the 19 tour lines in this browser's voice, one clip per line, so the tour can use the same voice on every device.

How to use it (about 5 minutes)
  1. Open this page on the laptop and browser where you like the voice. Check the voice name below.
  2. Turn the laptop volume up to a normal level. The recording takes the computer's sound directly, so room noise doesn't matter.
  3. Click Start capture. When the browser asks what to share, pick Entire screen, turn on Share system audio, then click Share. On a Mac, if there's no system audio option, choose the tab and turn on Share tab audio.
  4. Wait while it plays all 19 lines. Don't play other sounds.
  5. Click Download clips and send me the zip file.
